Prepare for Your First Nether Trip

Published October 15, 2026 by Anthony Combalicer

A Nether portal is a doorway into a hazardous dimension, so make your first visit a planned supply run rather than an open-ended expedition. Prepare at home, keep the portal area recognizable, and decide what you want to accomplish before you leave.

Prepare your equipment

Bring food, a shield, a durable pickaxe, blocks for bridging, and a bow or other ranged option if you have one. Carry a fire-resistance option if it is already available to you, but do not rely on finding one after entering. Leave irreplaceable items in a secure chest at home. Wear at least one piece of gold armor to reduce trouble with piglins, and avoid opening containers near them.

Make a safe portal landing

Before crossing, note the portal's location in the Overworld and clear a small area around it. After arriving, build a simple protective shell around the Nether-side portal using sturdy blocks you can safely place. Light the entrance and make the route recognizable with a consistent marker. Record coordinates or another clear landmark so you can find the return route even if you travel farther away.

Set a small first objective

Your first goal might be to inspect nearby terrain, collect a specific resource, or locate a safe route. Do not bridge over a drop without checking what lies below, and do not fight groups of hostile mobs in a narrow passage if retreat is possible. If your inventory fills, gear wears down, or the route becomes confusing, head back and store what you found. A short successful trip is better preparation for the next one.

  • Pack food, blocks, a shield, and useful tools.
  • Protect and mark both sides of the portal route.
  • Set one clear objective and return before supplies run low.
