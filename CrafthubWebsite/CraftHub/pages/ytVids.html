Before you start building

  • Gather double the blocks you think you need. Running out mid-build is the main reason people abandon a project.
  • Build in creative first if you are unsure. Copying it into survival afterwards is much faster than tearing down mistakes.
  • Light the inside as you go, or mobs will spawn in the rooms you have already finished.
